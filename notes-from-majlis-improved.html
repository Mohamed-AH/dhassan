A Window Into the Ocean

This platform serves as a digital companion for students seeking to benefit from the knowledge shared in Sheikh Hassan Dghriri's classes.

🎯 Our Purpose

To inspire and facilitate—not to replace—the authentic learning experience. Think of this as sitting in the back of a majlis, pen in hand, capturing the essence of what's being shared. We use AI to help organize and present these insights clearly.

📚 Our Methodology

Each lesson is carefully transcribed from the original Arabic recordings, then summarized using AI assistance. The notes capture key concepts, hadiths, and teachings while maintaining the spirit of the sheikh's explanations.

🌟 About the Sheikh

Sheikh Hassan Dghriri studied under the renowned Sheikh Ahmed bin Yahya an-Najmi (may Allah have mercy on him) and currently teaches from classical Islamic texts including Sahih Al-Bukhari's Book of Faith.
